About

Steady, and still learning

To work in an environment that encourages steady growth — professionally and personally — while putting skill and knowledge to honest use.

I'm a clinical psychologist working across counseling, psychological assessment, and therapeutic intervention with people from many walks of life. My practice centers on evidence-based approaches — Cognitive Behaviour Therapy (CBT) and client-centered counseling — alongside careful case history taking and Mental Status Examination (MSE).

Ethics and confidentiality aren't a formality to me; they're the ground a client stands on. Every session is held with care, structure, and respect for wherever someone is starting from.
